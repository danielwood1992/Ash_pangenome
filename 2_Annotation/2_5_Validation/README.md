### Validating the BATG-1.0 annotation

#### 2_5_1_BUSCO.sh - runs BUSCO on the final amino acid list for BATG-1.0
#### 2_5_2_Orthofinder.sh - runs Orthofinder on predicted amino acids, along with BATG-0.5 predicted proteins and other members of the Oleaceae
#### 2_5_6_OrthogroupStats.R - calculates thenumber of BATG-1.0 and BATG-0.5 genes that don't have genes assigned outside that assembly
