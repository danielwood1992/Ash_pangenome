### Validating the BATG-1.0 annotation

#### NC_2_5_1_BUSCO.sh - runs BUSCO on the final amino acid list for BATG-1.0
#### NC_2_5_2_Orthofinder.sh - runs Orthofinder on predicted amino acids, along with BATG-0.5 predicted proteins and other members of the Oleaceae
#### NC_2_5_3_OrthogroupStats.R - calculates thenumber of BATG-1.0 and BATG-0.5 genes that don't have genes assigned outside that assembly
#### NC_2_5_4_transdecoder.sh - runs transdecoder
